Privacy policy

Keep this page transparent so users know what data is collected, why it is used, and how it is handled.

Dino Bot Privacy Policy

Dino Bot may process limited Discord data required to operate properly, such as user IDs, server IDs, message content used for moderation commands, and command interaction details.

  • Data is used only to provide bot features, moderation, logging, and support.
  • Data should not be sold or shared for advertising purposes.
  • Temporary data may be stored for logs, cooldowns, or anti-spam protection.
  • Server admins may request removal of stored bot data where applicable.
  • Users should not submit sensitive personal information through bot commands.

Dino Bot Terms of Service

By using Dino Bot, users agree to follow Discord’s rules, community standards, and the server’s own policies. Abuse, harassment, exploit attempts, or malicious use of the bot may result in restriction or removal.

  • The bot is provided “as is” without guarantee of uninterrupted service.
  • Features may change, be removed, or be added at any time.
  • Owners may limit access to certain commands or features.
  • Misuse of moderation or utility commands can lead to penalties.
  • Use of the bot implies acceptance of these terms.

Server rules

These rules help keep the community safe, organized, and fun for everyone.

  • Be respectful to everyone.
  • No spam, flooding, or unnecessary pings.
  • Keep content in the correct channels.
  • No harassment, hate speech, or abusive behavior.
  • No NSFW content unless a channel is explicitly allowed for it.
  • Do not impersonate staff or misuse bot commands.
  • Follow Discord’s Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
